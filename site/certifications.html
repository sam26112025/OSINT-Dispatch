■ Entry Level Certifications
CompTIA Security+
CompTIA Security+ SY0-701
BeginnerVendor Neutral
The global baseline certification for cybersecurity. Covers network security, cryptography, threats, vulnerabilities, risk management, and identity concepts. Most employers require this as a minimum for SOC roles. No prerequisites — ideal first certification.
💰 India salary range: Rs 4–10 LPA (entry SOC analyst)
Exam: SY0-701 · 90 questions · 90 min · ~$380 USD

CEH v12
Certified Ethical Hacker — EC-Council
Beginner-IntermediateIndia Popular
The most recognized certification in Indian government, law enforcement, and defence contexts. Covers hacking methodology, tools, and techniques from an ethical hacker perspective. EC-Council is India-headquartered — high recognition with domestic employers including MHA, defence PSUs, and cyber cells.
💰 India salary range: Rs 5–15 LPA
Exam: 312-50 · 125 questions · 4 hours · ~$950 USD

IntermediateLaw Enforcement
The primary digital forensics certification for Indian law enforcement personnel. Covers computer forensics investigation process, evidence collection, Windows/Linux/mobile forensics, network forensics, dark web investigations, and reporting. Required by many state cyber cells.

■ Advanced Certifications
OSCP
Offensive Security Certified Professional
AdvancedGold Standard
The most respected penetration testing certification globally. 24-hour practical exam — hack into machines, write a professional report. No multiple choice, no shortcuts. OSCP on a resume immediately separates genuine technical talent from paper certifications. Demanding but transformative.
💰 India salary range: Rs 15–40 LPA
Exam: 24hr practical + 24hr report · ~$1499 USD (with 90-day lab)
Free Study Resources
GitHub: OSCP Preparation Guide (Community) HackTheBox OSCP-prep machine lists TryHackMe OSCP Preparation Path
CISM
Certified Information Security Manager — ISACA
AdvancedManagement Track
Management-track certification for senior security professionals moving into CISO and leadership roles. Covers information security governance, risk management, program development, and incident management. 5 years experience required. High demand in Indian BFSI and large enterprise sectors.
💰 India salary range: Rs 20–60 LPA (CISO level)
Exam: 150 questions · 4 hours · ~$575 USD (member)

Choosing a Certification Path

Certifications are useful as a structured way to build and signal capability, but they are means rather than ends. For professionals working in OSINT, digital forensics, and cyber investigations in India, the right path depends on your role: investigators benefit most from credentials that emphasise evidence handling and lawful procedure, while offensive and defensive analysts are served by hands-on, practical examinations over multiple-choice formats.

Weigh a certification by the realism of its assessment, its recognition among the agencies and employers you intend to work with, and the currency of its content in a field that changes quickly. Pair any credential with demonstrable practical work — documented investigations, contributions, and continuous study — because in this profession a portfolio of defensible work ultimately carries more weight than a certificate alone.
