MISSION

"Every investigator deserves access to the same intelligence resources — regardless of budget, rank, or organisation size. IGRS exists to democratise cybersecurity intelligence for India's frontline defenders."

India's cybercrime landscape is evolving faster than most organisations can track. UPI fraud, digital arrest scams, SIM swap attacks, APT campaigns targeting critical infrastructure — the threats are real, sophisticated, and growing. Yet the tools and knowledge to fight them remain scattered, expensive, or simply inaccessible to most investigators.

IGRS was built to change that. One platform. Everything free. Built for the field, not the conference room.

Our Approach

IGRS exists to close a practical gap: capable open-source intelligence and forensics resources have long been scattered, expensive, or written for audiences outside India. This platform consolidates them into a single, free, field-ready reference grounded in Indian law and investigative practice, so that a cyber-cell officer, a corporate security analyst, or a student can find what they need without an account, a subscription, or a steep learning curve.

The content is written from a practitioner’s perspective — correlating fragmented data into structured intelligence, building defensible evidence chains, and translating technical findings into briefings that decision-makers can act on. Every tool, operator, and playbook here is intended strictly for lawful, authorised use, and the legal references throughout are kept current with the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, and Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam of 2023.
