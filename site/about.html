About IGRS

IGRS is an independent publication focused on open-source intelligence (OSINT), digital forensics, online privacy and cybersecurity awareness. The goal is simple: take subjects that are usually wrapped in jargon or sensationalism and explain them accurately, from the perspective of someone who does this work professionally.

What we publish

  • OSINT methodology — how open-source research actually works, its legal boundaries and its ethical limits.
  • Digital safety guides — practical, step-by-step instructions for protecting personal data, social media accounts and devices.
  • Digital forensics and evidence — how digital evidence is collected, preserved and treated by courts, explained for students and general readers.
  • Career guidance — honest information about certifications, roles and skill paths in cybersecurity and forensics in India.

What we do not publish

This site does not publish hacking tutorials, exploitation techniques, instructions for accessing private data, or any content intended to facilitate unauthorised access or surveillance. Every article is written to be defensive, educational and lawful. Where investigative methods are discussed, they are described at the level of public understanding, not operational tradecraft.

About the author

Manuj Kumar Pandey is a senior cybersecurity professional with 6+ years of experience in OSINT investigations, digital forensics and threat intelligence. He is certified in CEH, CHFI and ECSA, and works with law enforcement agencies and enterprise security teams in India. Everything published here is educational and based on hands-on investigative practice.

Professional certifications held by the author include Certified Ethical Hacker (CEH), Computer Hacking Forensic Investigator (CHFI) and EC-Council Certified Security Analyst (ECSA). He has produced court-admissible forensic and intelligence reports and has trained law-enforcement personnel on digital investigation workflows.

Editorial standards

All articles are original, written from first-hand professional experience, and reviewed for factual accuracy before publication. When laws or statistics are cited, they are checked against primary sources such as government portals and official statutes. If you spot an error, please use the contact page — corrections are made promptly and transparently.
