Cryptocurrency Fraud Investigation in India — Tracing and Recovery Guide

Cryptocurrency fraud in India has grown exponentially — from fake investment schemes to pig butchering scams to NFT fraud. Despite the pseudonymous nature of blockchain transactions, cryptocurrencies leave permanent, traceable records. This guide covers the investigation methodology.

Common Crypto Fraud Types in India

  • Fake investment platforms: Victims shown fake returns, then locked out when withdrawing
  • Pig butchering: Long-term relationship fraud ending in crypto investment
  • NFT fraud: Fake NFT projects, rug pulls
  • Mining scams: Pay to join fake mining pool
  • Recovery scam: Fraudsters posing as recovery agents for previous crypto fraud victims

Step 1: Blockchain Analysis

Every Bitcoin/Ethereum transaction is permanently recorded on the public blockchain. Free tools for tracing:

  • blockchain.com/explorer — Bitcoin transaction lookup
  • etherscan.io — Ethereum and ERC-20 token tracing
  • bscscan.com — Binance Smart Chain
  • tronscan.org — Tron/USDT-TRC20 (most Indian fraud uses this)

Follow the transaction trail: victim wallet → suspect wallet → exchange deposit address

Step 2: Exchange Identification

Most crypto eventually reaches a centralized exchange (CEX) for conversion to fiat. Indian exchanges with KYC compliance:

  • WazirX, CoinDCX, Zebpay, Binance India — registered with FIU-IND
  • International: Binance, Coinbase, Kraken — require MLAT or international legal process

Step 3: Legal Process for Exchange KYC

  • Indian exchanges: Section 91 BNSS notice + complaint number from cybercrime.gov.in
  • FIU-IND (Financial Intelligence Unit) — report suspicious crypto transactions at fiuindia.gov.in
  • PMLA Section 3/4 applies to money laundering through crypto
  • International exchanges: MLAT request through CBI or MEA

Frequently Asked Questions

Can cryptocurrency fraud be traced in India?
Yes. All blockchain transactions are permanently recorded and publicly visible. Investigators can trace Bitcoin, Ethereum, and USDT transactions to exchange deposit addresses, then request KYC data from the exchange using legal process.
How do I report cryptocurrency fraud in India?
Report to cybercrime.gov.in and call 1930. Also report to FIU-IND (Financial Intelligence Unit) at fiuindia.gov.in for money laundering aspects. Applicable sections include PMLA Section 3/4 and BNS Section 318.
Which law governs cryptocurrency in India?
Cryptocurrency is taxed under Income Tax Act (30% flat tax on gains). Crypto exchanges must register with FIU-IND. PMLA provisions apply to money laundering through crypto. RBI's CBDC (Digital Rupee) operates under RBI Act.
