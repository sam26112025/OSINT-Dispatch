SIM Swap Fraud Investigation in India — Complete Guide 2026

SIM swap fraud is one of the most sophisticated forms of mobile banking fraud in India. By fraudulently porting a victim's mobile number to a new SIM, criminals gain access to all OTPs, bypassing two-factor authentication for banking, UPI, and email accounts. This guide covers complete investigation methodology.

How SIM Swap Fraud Works

  1. Intelligence gathering: Criminals obtain victim's Aadhaar, DOB, and address through social engineering, data breach, or insider at bank/telecom
  2. Port request: Criminals visit telecom retailer (often with forged Aadhaar/ID) and request SIM porting or replacement
  3. OTP interception: Once new SIM is active, all OTPs go to criminal
  4. Account takeover: Criminal resets passwords, transfers funds, changes contact details
  5. Victim discovery: Victim discovers when their SIM stops working or receives bank alerts

Investigation Methodology

Immediate Steps

  • Victim should immediately call telecom operator to block the ported SIM
  • Contact bank to block all transactions
  • Call 1930 and file complaint

Evidence Collection

  • Telecom operator port request logs — who submitted the port request, from which location
  • Aadhaar verification logs — was victim's Aadhaar used, was biometric used
  • CCTV from the telecom retailer where port request was made
  • Bank transaction logs — all transactions after SIM swap

OSINT Investigation

  • TAFCOP — verify which operator issued new SIM
  • Telecom retailer identification from DoT records
  • CCTV from retailer — suspect biometric photograph
  • MNPVS (Mobile Number Portability Voucher System) logs via DoT LEA

Telecom Operator and Bank Liability

RBI circular RBI/2019-20/142 holds banks liable for unauthorized transactions in SIM swap cases if the bank did not take adequate precautions. Banks must refund within 7 working days. Telecom operators face liability under Consumer Protection Act if port was issued fraudulently.

FIR Sections

S.66C IT Act (identity theft), S.66D IT Act (personation using computer), S.318 BNS (cheating), S.319 BNS (personation), and additionally S.468 IPC (if case pre-July 2024) for forgery of documents used in port request.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does SIM swap fraud happen in India?
Criminals gather your personal data, then visit a telecom retailer with forged ID to request SIM porting. Once activated, they receive all your OTPs and take over your bank accounts, UPI, and email. Your original SIM stops working as the first sign.
How to detect SIM swap fraud?
Your mobile phone loses signal suddenly. You stop receiving calls and SMS. You receive unexpected OTPs or bank alerts. Check sancharsaathi.gov.in to see if a new SIM was issued on your Aadhaar without your knowledge.
Can bank refund money lost in SIM swap fraud?
Yes. RBI circular 2019-20/142 holds banks liable for SIM swap fraud if proper security measures weren't taken. File a complaint with your bank immediately, then approach RBI Ombudsman if the bank does not refund within 7 working days.
