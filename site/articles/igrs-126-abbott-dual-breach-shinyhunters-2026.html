Abbott Labs July 2026: ShinyHunters + ShadowByt3$ Claim Dual Breach — 30M Records, 1M+ SSNs, Medical Data

By Manuj Kumar Pandey27 July 2026Global Cyber IntelligenceHealthcare BreachShinyHunters30M Records

Abbott Laboratories suffered two simultaneous data breaches in July 2026 — claimed by ShinyHunters (Cancer Diagnostics unit) and ShadowByt3$ (LabCenter portal). ShinyHunters claims 30 million customer rows including 1M+ SSNs and 22M medical client notes. Abbott India Limited (NSE listed) may be affected.

What data was stolen in the Abbott breach?

Who is ShinyHunters in 2026?
