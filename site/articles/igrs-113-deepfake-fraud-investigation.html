Detection Methodology: Tools & Techniques

Tier 1 — AI-Based Detection Tools

Microsoft Video Authenticator
Frame-by-frame confidence score. Detects GAN blending artifacts. Good for static deepfakes.
Intel FakeCatcher
Real-time detection via rPPG (remote photoplethysmography) — analyses blood vessel micro-patterns invisible to human eye. 96% accuracy on uncompressed video.
FaceForensics++
Open-source CNN model trained on face swap + face reenactment. Best for research-grade analysis. GitHub: ondyari/FaceForensics
Hive Moderation API
Platform-scale deepfake detection. Used by I4C + Microsoft partnership for batch processing of flagged content. REST API.
Deepware Scanner
Free online deepfake scanner. Upload video → confidence score + artifact map. Good for field-level quick check. deepware.ai
Sensity AI
Enterprise deepfake detection platform. Covers video + audio + image. Used by financial institutions for KYC liveness fraud detection.

Critical limitation: All AI detectors lose accuracy post-compression (WhatsApp/Telegram compress videos). Accuracy drops ~25% on compressed content. Always request original uncompressed file from platform via legal process for reliable forensic analysis.

Landmark Cases: Delhi HC Jurisprudence

Ankur Warikoo v John Doe (Delhi HC, 2025)

First landmark deepfake case in India's financial fraud context. AI-generated videos using Warikoo's face and voice were used to lure victims into fraudulent WhatsApp stock tip groups. Delhi HC issued a John Doe order — injunction against unknown defendants — relying on personality rights framework from Shivaji Rao Gaikwad v Varsha Productions. This established that personality rights extend to AI-generated misuse of likeness, voice, and identity.

Digital Arrest Syndicate (Delhi, 2025)

50-member organised syndicate used AI-cloned police officer faces for WhatsApp video arrests — extorted ₹500 crore. MCOCA (Maharashtra Control of Organised Crime Act) invoked. Special Court granted 90-day custody. Bail denied under Section 21 MCOCA citing public safety. This case established that AI-enabled organised crime qualifies for MCOCA application.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which IT Act sections apply to deepfake fraud?
S.66E (privacy violation), S.67 (obscene content), S.67A (explicit content), S.319 BNS (personation), S.308 BNS (extortion for sextortion), S.318 BNS (cheating). IT Rules 2026 add platform liability for non-removal within 2-3 hours.
How serious is deepfake fraud in India?
92,000+ deepfake digital arrest incidents in 2025. ₹3,000 crore in digital arrest losses alone. Ambani/Tata/Murthy/Kohli deepfakes used in investment scams. Deepfake fraud grew 2,137% in 3 years. A 60-second deepfake now costs zero and takes under 25 minutes.
How do investigators detect deepfakes?
Intel FakeCatcher (rPPG blood vessel analysis), FaceForensics++ (GAN artifact detection), Microsoft Video Authenticator, Deepware Scanner. Manual: check blinking, hairline artifacts, audio sync, lighting consistency. Always use uncompressed original — compression reduces detection accuracy by 25%.
What is the Delhi HC John Doe order for deepfakes?
Ankur Warikoo v John Doe (2025) — Delhi HC granted injunction against unknown creators of AI deepfake financial scam videos using Warikoo's face/voice. First case applying personality rights to deepfake fraud in India. Paves way for proactive John Doe orders in celebrity deepfake fraud.
