TAFCOP Portal — Complete Guide for Law Enforcement Investigators

TAFCOP — Telecom Analytics for Fraud Management and Consumer Protection — is one of India's most powerful and underutilised investigative tools. Hosted at sancharsaathi.gov.in, it allows citizens and investigators to check how many SIM cards are registered against any Aadhaar number, report fraudulent connections, and track telecom-related fraud. For cybercrime investigators, it is a critical first-stop for any case involving mobile numbers.

What TAFCOP Can Tell You

  • How many mobile connections are registered under a suspect's Aadhaar
  • Which telecom operator issued each SIM
  • Whether connections have been reported as fraudulent
  • Whether a mobile number is active or inactive

Accessing TAFCOP

Public portal: sancharsaathi.gov.in/telecom/MH/tafcop.jsp

The public portal allows any mobile subscriber to check connections registered to their own mobile number. For investigators requiring Aadhaar-linked lookups, the portal operates through DoT's Law Enforcement Agency (LEA) interface, accessible through your state's cyber cell or DoT regional office.

Step-by-Step: Public Portal Investigation

  1. Navigate to sancharsaathi.gov.in
  2. Select "Know Your Mobile Connections"
  3. Enter the mobile number under investigation
  4. Enter the OTP sent to that number (requires access to the device/SIM)
  5. Review all connections registered to the linked Aadhaar
  6. Report suspicious connections directly from the portal

LEA Portal — Enhanced Access

Law enforcement agencies have access to an enhanced DoT portal through which investigators can:

  • Query connections by Aadhaar number (not just mobile number)
  • Request CDR (Call Detail Records) through the portal
  • File requests for SIM block/suspension
  • Access subscriber verification data

Access to the LEA portal requires a letter from the SP or higher rank officer through the state's nodal officer to DoT.

Key Use Cases in Investigation

SIM Swap Fraud Investigation

When a victim reports unauthorized SIM swap, TAFCOP reveals whether multiple SIMs were issued on the same Aadhaar within a short timeframe — a key indicator of fraudulent activity at the telecom retailer level.

Digital Arrest Scam

Fraudsters often use SIMs registered on others' Aadhaar. TAFCOP verification quickly reveals whether the SIM used by the suspect belongs to a genuine subscriber or a mule Aadhaar.

OTP Fraud

Criminals who obtain unauthorized OTPs often use SIM farming — multiple SIMs on one Aadhaar. TAFCOP identifies SIM farms instantly.

Legal Authority

CDR and subscriber data requests must be accompanied by a written order under Section 92 BNSS (formerly Section 91 CrPC). For real-time interception, an order under Section 5(2) of the Indian Telegraph Act 1885 is required, issued by the Home Secretary or authorized officer.

Conclusion

TAFCOP should be the first tool any cybercrime investigator reaches for when a mobile number is involved. Combined with CDR analysis and UPI transaction trails, it provides a powerful foundation for building the suspect identification chain.
