Phishing Email Investigation — Complete Guide for India 2026

Phishing emails are the entry point for over 90% of cyber attacks in India. From CEO fraud to tax refund scams, phishing email investigation is a core skill for any cybercrime investigator. This guide walks through the complete methodology.

Step 1: Email Header Analysis

Email headers contain the full path of an email — from sender's mail server to your inbox. Access headers in Gmail: three dots → "Show original". In Outlook: File → Properties.

Key fields to examine:

  • Return-Path: Where bounced emails go — often reveals real sender domain
  • Received: from — chain of mail servers, read bottom to top
  • X-Originating-IP: Sender's IP address
  • Authentication-Results: SPF, DKIM, DMARC pass/fail
  • Message-ID: Unique identifier for tracking

Step 2: IP Address Investigation

Take the originating IP from headers and check:

  • VirusTotal — malicious activity history
  • AbuseIPDB — reported abuse
  • Shodan — open ports, server type
  • IPInfo — geolocation, ASN, ISP
  • IGRS Dashboard — check.igrs.xyz for quick lookup

Step 3: Domain Investigation

  • WHOIS — registration date, registrar, name servers
  • crt.sh — SSL certificates, related subdomains
  • URLScan — screenshot, links, technologies
  • PhishTank — check if domain is known phishing
  • VirusTotal — malware and phishing history

Step 4: Reporting

  • CERT-In: incident@cert-in.org.in — mandatory for critical infrastructure
  • Phishtank: phishtank.org — public phishing database
  • Google Safe Browsing: safebrowsing.google.com/safebrowsing/report_phish
  • Cybercrime.gov.in: for financial fraud phishing

Applicable Legal Sections

Section 66 IT Act (computer related offences), Section 66D IT Act (personation using computer), Section 318 BNS (cheating), and Section 74 IT Act (publication for fraudulent purpose).

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I trace a phishing email in India?
Analyze the email headers to extract the originating IP address. Check the IP on VirusTotal, AbuseIPDB, and IPInfo. Investigate the sending domain using WHOIS and crt.sh. Report to CERT-In at incident@cert-in.org.in and cybercrime.gov.in.
How do I view email headers in Gmail for phishing investigation?
In Gmail, open the phishing email, click the three dots (⋮) menu, select 'Show original'. This displays the full email headers including originating IP, mail server chain, and authentication results.
What should I report a phishing email in India?
Report to CERT-In (incident@cert-in.org.in), PhishTank (phishtank.org), Google Safe Browsing (safebrowsing.google.com/safebrowsing/report_phish), and cybercrime.gov.in if financial fraud was involved.
