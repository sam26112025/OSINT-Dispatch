Cyber Insurance India — Complete Guide for Businesses 2026

Cyber insurance is becoming essential for Indian businesses as cyberattacks increase. India witnessed over 1.35 billion data records breached in 2024, with average breach costs exceeding ₹19 crore. This guide covers everything businesses need to know about cyber insurance in India.

What Cyber Insurance Covers in India

  • Data breach response: Forensic investigation, notification costs, credit monitoring
  • Business interruption: Revenue loss during ransomware downtime
  • Cyber extortion: Ransomware payment (controversial but available)
  • Third-party liability: Customer claims for data breach
  • Regulatory fines: DPDP Act penalties (limited coverage)
  • Social engineering fraud: CEO fraud, BEC attacks

Key Providers in India

Major insurers offering cyber policies in India include ICICI Lombard, Bajaj Allianz, HDFC ERGO, Tata AIG, New India Assurance, and international players like AXA XL and Zurich. Premiums vary significantly based on industry, revenue, and security posture.

What is NOT Covered

  • War and state-sponsored cyberattacks (exclusion expanding post-Russia-Ukraine)
  • Prior known vulnerabilities not patched
  • Intentional acts by insured party
  • Bodily injury from cyber events
  • Infrastructure failure (power grid, internet outage)

IRDAI Regulation

IRDAI (Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India) regulates cyber insurance products. As of 2026, IRDAI has mandated standardized policy wordings for key coverage elements to reduce disputes. Always verify that your policy complies with current IRDAI guidelines.

Before Buying: Security Posture Assessment

Insurers now require detailed security questionnaires covering: MFA deployment, patch management, backup procedures, endpoint security, employee training, and incident response plans. Weak security posture results in higher premiums or coverage denial.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does cyber insurance cover in India?
Cyber insurance in India typically covers data breach response costs, business interruption, cyber extortion, third-party liability, regulatory fines under DPDP Act, and social engineering fraud. Coverage varies by policy.
Is cyber insurance mandatory in India?
Cyber insurance is not currently mandatory in India, but SEBI requires listed companies to disclose cyber risks. RBI guidelines for banks and IRDAI for insurers strongly recommend cyber coverage. It is expected to become mandatory for critical sectors.
How much does cyber insurance cost in India?
Cyber insurance premiums in India range from ₹50,000 per year for SMBs to crores for large enterprises. Premium depends on revenue, industry, data sensitivity, and security posture. Healthcare and financial services pay highest premiums.
