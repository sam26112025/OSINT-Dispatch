India Ransomware Crisis 2026: +31.4% YoY — CERT-In Logs 29.44 Lakh Incidents, Losses Cross ₹20,000 Crore

By Manuj Kumar Pandey27 July 2026Threat IntelligenceRansomware +31.4%29.44 Lakh Incidents₹20K Crore

CERT-In logged 29.44 lakh cyber incidents in 2025 — nearly three million in a single year. Ransomware increased 31.4% year-on-year. Total cyber losses crossed ₹20,000 crore. The ransomware ecosystem has fragmented: smaller RaaS groups replace large syndicates, causing a 53% increase in extortion victims.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many cyber incidents did CERT-In log in 2025?
CERT-In logged 29.44 lakh (2,944,000) cyber incidents in 2025 — nearly three million. Additionally, 1,530 alerts and 65 advisories were issued covering critical threat patterns.
Which ransomware groups are targeting India?
Sinobi, Bashe, LockBit 3.0, ShinyHunters, and DragonForce are active against Indian targets. Most operate RaaS models. Sinobi claimed an India IT services company in January 2026.
What is the mandatory ransomware reporting requirement?
CERT-In Directions 2022: report within 6 hours. DPDP Act 2023 (May 2027): notify Data Protection Board within 72 hours if personal data involved. Failure: ₹1 lakh/day CERT-In fine + up to ₹200 crore DPDP penalty.
Why is cloud misconfiguration the top vector?
DSCI telemetry shows 62% of Indian breaches occur in cloud. Exposed S3/Blob buckets, excessive IAM permissions, and absent CSPM let attackers access data without touching the network perimeter.
