Mobile Banking Fraud Investigation in India — Complete Guide

Mobile banking fraud costs Indian consumers thousands of crores annually. From sophisticated vishing attacks to OTP phishing to insider banking fraud, the attack surface is vast. This guide covers investigation methodology for the most common mobile banking fraud types.

Common Mobile Banking Fraud Types

  • Vishing: Phone calls impersonating bank officials, extracting OTP/credentials
  • Screen sharing fraud: Victim downloads AnyDesk/TeamViewer on fraud pretense, criminal controls device
  • OTP phishing: Fake SMS/email links leading to credential-harvesting pages
  • Insider fraud: Bank employees using customer data for unauthorized transfers
  • Card not present (CNP) fraud: Online transactions using stolen card details

Investigation Methodology

Immediate Evidence Collection

  • Bank statement with all unauthorized transactions and UTR numbers
  • Call recordings if victim recorded the call
  • SMS received — note exact text, sender ID, timestamp
  • Any links clicked — preserve URL, check on URLScan
  • App download logs if screen sharing app was installed

Banking Channel Investigation

  • Issue Section 91 BNSS to victim's bank for complete transaction trail
  • IP address of login session — ISP records for location
  • Device fingerprint — bank logs capture device ID
  • Beneficiary account details — issue Section 91 to receiving bank

Telecom Evidence

  • CDR for the fraudster's calling number
  • TAFCOP to identify Aadhaar linked to the number
  • Cell tower data for physical location during fraud call

Bank Liability under RBI Guidelines

Under RBI Master Direction on Customer Protection 2019:

  • Zero liability if fraud reported within 3 working days and not due to customer negligence
  • ₹10,000 maximum liability if reported within 4-7 days
  • Customer bears full loss if more than 7 days delay
  • Bank must refund within 10 working days of complaint

Applicable Legal Sections

S.66C IT Act (identity theft), S.66D IT Act (personation using computer), S.318 BNS (cheating), S.43A IT Act (compensation for failure to protect data — against bank if insider fraud).

Frequently Asked Questions

What to do immediately after mobile banking fraud in India?
Call your bank's 24x7 fraud helpline immediately to block the account. Call 1930 and file complaint on cybercrime.gov.in. Get a complaint acknowledgment number and use it to request account freeze at the receiving bank. Time is critical — freeze must happen before funds are withdrawn.
Is the bank liable for mobile banking fraud in India?
Yes, under RBI Master Direction 2019. If you report within 3 working days and the fraud was not due to your negligence, the bank has zero liability and must refund. The refund window is 10 working days. Approach RBI Ombudsman if the bank refuses.
How do banks investigate mobile banking fraud in India?
Banks investigate using internal fraud detection systems, device fingerprinting, IP geolocation, and transaction velocity analysis. For criminal investigation, they provide records in response to police/court orders under Section 91 BNSS.
