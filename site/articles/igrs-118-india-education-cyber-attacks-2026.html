India Education Sector Under Siege: 7,684 Cyber Attacks Per Week — Most Targeted Globally

By Manuj Kumar Pandey27 July 2026Threat IntelligenceEducation #1 Target7684 Attacks/WeekRansomware

India's education sector faces 7,684 cyber attacks per organisation every week — the highest of any sector globally per Check Point's 2026 report. Universities, ed-tech platforms, state boards and IITs are all targeted due to weak budgets, legacy systems, and massive student PII repositories.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is education the most attacked sector in India?
Limited cybersecurity budgets, legacy systems, large student PII repositories (Aadhaar, PAN, health records), thousands of phishing-susceptible users, and high ransomware leverage during exam seasons.
What was the Canvas LMS breach?
ShinyHunters breached Instructure's Canvas LMS in May 2026, exfiltrating 3.65TB from 275 million users across 9,000 institutions globally. Indian universities using Canvas were potentially affected.
What CERT-In reporting applies to educational institutions?
All cyber incidents must be reported within 6 hours of detection under CERT-In Directions 2022. DPDP Act 2023 adds 72-hour Data Protection Board notification and up to ₹250 crore penalty for student data breaches from May 2027.
What can institutions do right now?
Conduct vulnerability assessment of all public-facing portals, implement MFA for admin accounts, patch all systems, encrypt student databases, and register with CERT-In's constituency portal.
