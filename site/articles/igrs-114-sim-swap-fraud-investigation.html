SIM Swap Fraud Investigation: TRAI MNP, Telecom Nodal Officer Process & Complete LEA Guide

By Manuj Kumar Pandey5 July 2026 Telecom Fraud Investigation MNP Hijack Insider Telecom Fraud S.66C IT Act

SIM swap fraud is deceptively simple — a criminal convinces a telecom operator to hand over a victim's phone number, and instantly they receive every OTP from their bank, UPI, Demat, email, Aadhaar, and IT department. The victim's phone goes silent. Their financial life is drained before they realise what happened. In 2024, IDCARE reported a 240% surge in SIM swap cases globally, with 90% occurring without any victim interaction. India, with its Aadhaar-linked telecom ecosystem, has unique attack vectors that demand a specific investigation methodology.

How SIM Swap Fraud Works in India: 4 Attack Vectors

Attack VectorMethodEnabler
Customer care impersonationCriminal calls telecom, claims "lost SIM, need replacement" — provides leaked KYC data (name, DOB, Aadhaar last 4 digits) to pass verificationData breaches + dark web KYC data
Insider/PoS retailer fraudCorrupt telecom franchisee or employee issues duplicate SIM to criminal for ₹5,000–₹50,000 bribeWeak franchisee oversight by operators
e-KYC Aadhaar biometric abuseCriminal uses cloned fingerprint (from sub-registrar leaks, e-KYC scanner traps) to authenticate duplicate SIM via Aadhaar OTPBiometric data theft from registrar offices
MNP port-out fraudCriminal initiates port to different network using stolen UPC code — moves number out of original operator entirelyStolen UPC + porting OTP interception

Post-swap impact: Victim receives no signal. Attacker intercepts OTPs from bank, UPI (PhonePe/GPay/Paytm), Demat account, mutual funds, email 2FA, Aadhaar OTP, IT department refunds — complete digital identity takeover within minutes.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is SIM swap fraud and how does it work in India?
Criminal convinces telecom to issue new SIM for victim's number — via social engineering, corrupt employee bribe (₹5K–₹50K), cloned Aadhaar biometric, or MNP port-out. Post-swap: all OTPs (bank, UPI, Demat, email) go to attacker. Victim's phone goes silent. Account drained.
What should investigators request via S.94 BNSS?
From telecom nodal officer: SIM issuance log, Aadhaar authentication log, PoS retailer details + CCTV, UPC generation log (MNP cases), IMEI history, post-swap OTP SMS log. From bank: IP log, device fingerprint, UPI transaction chain. All responses need S.63 BSA 2023 certificate.
What is the TRAI 7-day rule?
From July 2025 — if new SIM issued for a number, that number cannot be ported to another network for 7 days. Closes the "new SIM → immediate port-out" attack chain that criminals used to evade investigation by moving to a different operator immediately after SIM swap.
Which legal sections apply to SIM swap fraud?
S.66C IT Act (identity theft), S.66D IT Act (personation using computer), S.319 BNS (cheating by personation), S.318 BNS (cheating), S.336(4) BNS (forgery of electronic records for fake KYC). Corrupt telecom employee: Prevention of Corruption Act. PMLA if laundering established.
