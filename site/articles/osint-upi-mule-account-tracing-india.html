OSINT for UPI Fraud — Tracing Mule Accounts in India

The Mule Account: The Engine of UPI Fraud

Behind virtually every UPI fraud in India lies a network of mule accounts — bank accounts used to receive and launder fraudulent proceeds. Tracing these accounts is the single most important skill in financial cybercrime investigation, because following the mule chain leads from the disposable foot soldiers to the organisers who profit. This guide details the complete OSINT and investigative methodology for tracing UPI mule accounts in India.

What Is a Mule Account?

A money mule is a person who allows their bank account to be used to receive and transfer fraud proceeds, usually in exchange for a small commission. Many mules are recruited through fake job advertisements and may not fully understand they are laundering money. Their accounts form the layers between the victim and the fraudster, deliberately structured to break the money trail.

The Three-Layer Mule Structure

LayerFunctionCharacteristics
Collector accountsReceive victim funds directlyOften genuine KYC, freshly recruited mules
Buffer accountsSplit and forward rapidlyMultiple transfers within minutes to break trail
Cash-out accountsFinal withdrawal pointATM, crypto conversion, or transfer to organisers

Understanding this structure is essential — freezing only the collector account recovers a fraction; mapping the full network reaches the organisers and the bulk of the funds.

VPA Analysis: Reading the UPI Handle

The Virtual Payment Address is rich with intelligence. The handle suffix reveals the Payment Service Provider — @oksbi indicates State Bank, @ybl points to PhonePe/Yes Bank, @paytm to Paytm Payments Bank. The prefix often embeds a phone number or name. Analysing VPAs across a fraud network reveals which PSPs the syndicate favours and provides correlation points for identity.

Obtaining Bank KYC and Statements

The legal backbone of mule tracing is the Section 94 BNSS notice served on the beneficiary bank. This compels disclosure of the account holder's KYC, the full transaction statement, and device/IP logs. The KYC reveals the mule's identity; the statement reveals where the money went next; the device logs reveal whether multiple mule accounts share a common operator device.

Link Analysis: Mapping the Network

With statements in hand, the investigator performs link analysis — treating each account as a node and each transfer as a connection. Patterns emerge: the same buffer account servicing multiple frauds, the same device logging into many accounts, funds converging on a single cash-out point. Tools like Maltego visualise these networks, transforming columns of transactions into a clear map of the syndicate.

Device Fingerprinting Across Mule Accounts

One of the most powerful pieces of evidence is the device fingerprint. Banks log the device ID and IP for each mobile banking session. When the same device ID appears across multiple supposedly-unrelated mule accounts, it is concrete proof of a coordinated operation run by a single operator — evidence that stands up strongly in court.

The Recruitment Pivot: From Mule to Organiser

The most valuable investigative insight is that mules are recruited, usually through fake job advertisements promising easy work-from-home income via "receive and forward payments" tasks. By tracing how a mule was recruited — the Telegram channel, WhatsApp group, or fake company — investigators reach the organising layer that recruits dozens or hundreds of mules. This pivot from the money trail to the recruitment trail is frequently what dismantles an entire syndicate rather than just arresting a disposable mule.

Phone and SIM Intelligence

Supporting the financial trail is phone intelligence:

  • TAFCOP checks on mule numbers reveal Aadhaar-linked SIM patterns
  • CDR analysis maps the mule's contact with recruiters and operators
  • Cross-referencing numbers across multiple mule accounts reveals coordination

Legal Framework

Section 318BNS 2023Cheating

Notably, even unknowing mules can face liability, though intent affects prosecution. The PMLA allows attachment of proceeds, and organised mule networks attract the full weight of money-laundering law.

Building the Case for Court

A mule-tracing investigation produces court-ready evidence when every bank record carries a Section 63 BSA 2023 certificate, hash values are computed at collection, and the link analysis clearly documents the flow from victim to organiser. The combination of financial trail, device fingerprinting, and recruitment evidence builds a case that not only convicts individual mules but exposes and dismantles the network behind them — the true objective of effective UPI mule investigation.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you trace UPI mule accounts in India?
Trace UPI mule accounts by analyzing VPA handles, requesting bank KYC via legal process, mapping the transaction chain from collector to cash-out, correlating phone numbers, and identifying recruitment patterns through fake job ads.
What is a mule account in UPI fraud?
A mule account is a bank account used to receive and forward fraud proceeds, often opened by people recruited through fake jobs who may not know they are laundering money. They form layers between victim and fraudster.
What legal sections apply to mule account operators?
Mule account operators face BNS Section 318 (cheating), IT Act Section 66D, and PMLA Sections 3/4 for money laundering. Even unknowing mules can face liability, though intent affects prosecution.
