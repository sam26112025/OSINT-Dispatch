UP Azamgarh: Pratibimb App Cracks ₹1,100 Crore Cyber Fraud Network — 60+ Arrested

By Manuj Kumar Pandey27 July 2026Indian State Cyber OperationsUP Police Op₹1100Cr BustedPratibimb App

On 24 July 2026, Uttar Pradesh's Azamgarh district police announced a landmark cyber fraud operation powered by the Pratibimb App — I4C's AI-based criminal location mapping system. The operation identified a fraud network with over ₹11,000 crore in linked transactions, resulting in 60+ arrests and ₹6 crore recovered for victims.

What is Pratibimb?

Pratibimb (Sanskrit: reflection) is a module launched by India's Indian Cyber Crime Coordination Centre (I4C) under the Union Home Ministry. It maps the locations of cyber criminals and crime infrastructure on a real-time geographic map, providing jurisdictional officers visibility into criminal hotspots. As of 2025, Pratibimb-led operations nationwide have resulted in over 6,046 accused arrested, 17,185 criminal linkages established, and 36,296 cyber investigation assistance requests processed.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Pratibimb App?
Pratibimb is I4C's (Indian Cyber Crime Coordination Centre) AI-based criminal location mapping system that tracks the geographic location of cybercriminals using mobile numbers, banking records, and digital footprints. It has enabled 6,046+ arrests and 36,296 investigation assistance requests nationwide.
How big was the Azamgarh cyber fraud network?
The Azamgarh operation identified a network with over ₹11,000 crore in linked fraudulent transactions. Over 60 accused were arrested and ₹6 crore was recovered for victims in the July 2026 operation.
What legal sections apply to these frauds?
S.318 BNS (cheating), S.319 BNS (cheating by personation), S.66C IT Act (identity theft), S.66D IT Act (personation using computer resource). For organised crime networks: PMLA S.3/4 for money laundering.
Where to report cyber fraud in India?
Call 1930 (national cyber helpline) or file on cybercrime.gov.in. Early reporting within 24 hours maximises chances of fund freeze via I4C's CFCFRMS platform.
