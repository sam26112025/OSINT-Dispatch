Job Fraud Investigation in India — OSINT and Legal Guide 2026

Job fraud is a massive and growing cybercrime in India, with victims losing lakhs to fake job offers from "MNCs," overseas employment scams, and mule recruitment operations. Job fraud also feeds other cybercrimes — mule accounts used in UPI fraud are recruited through fake job ads. This guide covers complete investigation methodology.

Types of Job Fraud in India

  • Fake MNC jobs: Emails from spoofed company domains offering high-salary positions, requesting registration/processing fees
  • Overseas employment fraud: Fake visa assistance, work permits, travel bookings for non-existent jobs
  • Part-time work fraud: Task completion fraud — pay to unlock tasks, never recover
  • Mule recruitment: "Receive and forward payments" — unknowing money laundering
  • Data entry fraud: Pay registration fee, work is unpaid or never materializes

OSINT Investigation Methodology

Company Verification

  • MCA21 search — is the company registered?
  • GST verification — does the GSTIN match claimed company?
  • Domain WHOIS — when was the website registered? Recent registration = fraud indicator
  • Google company name + "scam" / "fraud" / "complaint"
  • LinkedIn — does the company have real employees?

Job Portal Verification

  • Official job portals: Naukri, LinkedIn, Indeed, Monster — verify original listing
  • Fake portals often have domains like "naukri-india.com" or "linkedin-jobs.net"
  • Check SSL certificate age — newly issued = suspicious

Recruiter Identity Verification

  • Email header analysis — does email originate from claimed company domain?
  • Phone number OSINT — Truecaller, TAFCOP
  • LinkedIn profile age and connection count — fake profiles have few connections

Frequently Asked Questions

How to verify if a job offer from India is genuine?
Verify company registration on MCA21 (mca.gov.in), check GST number, search company name on Google with 'scam' or 'fraud', verify the recruiter's email domain matches the company website, and call the company's official number (from website, not email).
What are the FIR sections for job fraud in India?
Section 318 BNS (cheating), Section 319 BNS (personation), Section 66D IT Act (personation using computer). If the job fraud involved mule account recruitment, also add PMLA Section 3/4 for money laundering.
How to report fake job fraud in India?
Report to cybercrime.gov.in and call 1930. Also report the fake job posting directly to the platform (Naukri, LinkedIn, Indeed). For overseas job fraud involving visa fraud, report to Ministry of External Affairs at mea.gov.in.
