Detecting and Removing Stalkerware from Android and iOS Devices

File No.IGRS-031/2026
SubjectTechnical guide to identifying commercial surveillance software on personal devices
CategoryPrivacy & Safety
AuthorManuj Kumar Pandey
Published2026-05-23
Reading time6 minutes

Stalkerware is commercial spyware typically installed by an intimate partner or family member without consent. How to detect it, document it for legal purposes, and safely remove it.

What stalkerware is

Stalkerware refers to commercially sold mobile applications designed to monitor a device's activity — location, calls, messages, photos, and sometimes microphone and camera — while hiding from the device owner. It is distinct from parental control software in one critical way: it is designed to be covert. The user of the monitored device does not know it is installed. Stalkerware is most commonly installed by intimate partners in abusive relationships, by controlling family members, or by employers monitoring employees without proper disclosure. Its use without the device owner's knowledge or consent is illegal under the IT Act 2000 Section 66E (violation of privacy) and potentially Section 66C (identity theft) in India.

Indicators of stalkerware on Android

Android is more susceptible to stalkerware than iOS because it allows installation from outside the Play Store ("sideloading"). Warning signs include: battery draining faster than usual (stalkerware runs continuously in the background), data usage significantly higher than expected (it uploads captured data to a remote server), phone running hot without apparent cause, unusual permissions when you check Settings → Apps (a "calculator" app requesting microphone and location access is suspicious), and unfamiliar apps you do not remember installing. Android's Settings → Battery → Battery Usage shows which apps are consuming the most power — stalkerware typically appears in the top consumers despite you not using it.

Detection on iOS

Stalkerware is significantly harder to install on iOS without physical access and either the device's Apple ID credentials or a jailbreak. If someone has had unsupervised access to your unlocked iPhone, check: Settings → Privacy & Security → each permission category (location, microphone, camera, contacts) — look for apps that should not have these permissions; Settings → General → VPN & Device Management — any unexpected configuration profiles or MDM enrollment indicates monitoring capability; and unusual data usage in Settings → Mobile Data.

Documentation before removal

If you believe stalkerware is present and the situation involves domestic abuse or a legal dispute, document everything before removing it. Take screenshots of unusual apps, suspicious permissions, and battery/data usage. Note the date and time. If you are in a safe situation, preserve a backup of the device before factory resetting. This evidence may support a police complaint or protection order application. Coalition Against Stalkerware (stopstalkerware.org) provides guidance on working with law enforcement and support organizations internationally.

Safety first: If you are in a domestic abuse situation, removing stalkerware may alert the abuser. Consult a domestic abuse support organization before taking action — they can advise on safety planning around device monitoring. iCall India (9152987821) and iDare (idare.in) provide support.

Safe removal

The safest removal method for Android is a factory reset — this completely removes all installed applications including stalkerware. Back up only contacts and essential data (not apps or app data, which may reintroduce the stalkerware). For iOS, removing any unknown configuration profiles under Settings → General → VPN & Device Management and changing your Apple ID password (from a safe device) addresses most iOS-based monitoring. Change all passwords from a clean device after clearing the monitored phone.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is stalkerware?
Stalkerware is covert monitoring software installed on someone's device without consent to track location, read messages, monitor calls, and access data. It is often used in abusive relationships and is illegal in most contexts.
How do you detect stalkerware on a phone?
Signs include rapid battery drain, unusual data usage, the device running hot, unfamiliar apps, and settings changes. Anti-stalkerware tools, mobile security apps, and the MVT toolkit can detect known stalkerware.
How do you remove stalkerware safely?
Removal can alert the abuser, so safety planning comes first. Options include factory reset (after backing up evidence), using security tools, and seeking help from domestic violence support. Preserve evidence if pursuing legal action.
