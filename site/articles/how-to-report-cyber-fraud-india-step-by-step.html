How to Report Cyber Fraud in India — Complete Step by Step Guide 2026

Being defrauded online is traumatic — and the first few hours are critical for recovering money and building a case. This guide tells you exactly what to do, in what order, and who to contact.

Immediate Actions (First 30 Minutes)

The moment you realize you have been defrauded, take these steps immediately — order matters:

  1. Do NOT transfer any more money — disconnect from the fraudster
  2. Screenshot everything — WhatsApp chats, emails, payment confirmations, the fraudster's profile
  3. Note the UTR/Transaction ID of every fraudulent transaction
  4. Call 1930 — National Cyber Crime Helpline
  5. Call your bank's fraud helpline — request transaction freeze

Step 1: Call 1930 — National Cyber Crime Helpline

1930 is India's dedicated cybercrime helpline, operational 24x7. When you call:

  • Provide your name, location, and brief description of fraud
  • Give all transaction details — UTR numbers, beneficiary account, amount
  • The helpline operator will immediately contact the destination bank to flag the account
  • Note the complaint reference number given to you

Step 2: File Online Complaint at cybercrime.gov.in

  1. Visit cybercrime.gov.in
  2. Click "Report Cyber Crime" → "Report Financial Fraud"
  3. Register with your mobile number
  4. Fill complaint form: incident details, evidence upload, bank details
  5. Submit — receive acknowledgment number

This acknowledgment number is critical — use it when contacting your bank for freeze requests.

Step 3: Contact Your Bank

  • Call your bank's 24x7 fraud helpline with complaint number
  • Request "lien marking" or "hold" on the fraudulent transaction
  • If bank refuses: escalate to Branch Manager in writing
  • If still no action: complaint to RBI Ombudsman at rbi.org.in/Scripts/Complaints.aspx

Step 4: File FIR at Police Station

  • Visit nearest police station with cybercrime cell, or state cyber police
  • Carry: printouts of all evidence, bank statement, complaint acknowledgment number
  • Applicable sections: S.318 BNS, S.66C/66D IT Act
  • If FIR is refused: approach Superintendent of Police or Magistrate under S.175 BNSS

Step 5: Evidence to Preserve

  • WhatsApp/Telegram chats — export with media, hash immediately
  • Call recordings — if any
  • Bank statements — download PDF with digital signature
  • All URLs, domains, social media profiles of fraudster
  • Email headers — critical for email fraud cases

Recovery Expectations

Recovery depends on speed. If reported within 1 hour, chances are 60-70%. After 24 hours, chances drop significantly as money moves through mule account chains. The I4C (Indian Cyber Crime Coordination Centre) can flag accounts across banking networks, but only if reported promptly.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the cyber fraud helpline number in India?
1930 is India's National Cyber Crime Helpline, operational 24x7. For online complaints, visit cybercrime.gov.in. Both must be used immediately after discovering fraud.
How long does it take to get money back from cyber fraud?
If reported within hours, bank may be able to freeze funds before dispersal. Full recovery typically takes 3-6 months through legal process. Many cases recover partial amounts. Early reporting is critical.
Can I get my money back if I was scammed online in India?
Yes, recovery is possible especially for UPI and banking fraud if reported quickly via 1930 and cybercrime.gov.in. The RBI mandate requires banks to cooperate with freeze requests from law enforcement within 4 hours.
Is cybercrime.gov.in complaint legally valid?
Yes. A complaint on cybercrime.gov.in is legally valid and is forwarded to your local cyber cell for FIR registration. The acknowledgment number is accepted by banks and courts.
