ShinyHunters 2026: The Most Dangerous Ransomware Brand of the Year — 7 Major Breaches, 300M+ Records

By Manuj Kumar Pandey27 July 2026Threat IntelligenceShinyHunters300M+ RecordsRaaS Brand

ShinyHunters has emerged as the defining ransomware brand of 2026 — responsible for seven major verified breaches affecting 300+ million individuals, deploying an Oracle PeopleSoft zero-day, and operating a pay-or-leak extortion model that has extracted tens of millions in ransoms. Researchers describe ShinyHunters not as a single group, but as a durable cybercrime franchise — with multiple affiliate teams operating under the brand.

Oracle PeopleSoft zero-day: ShinyHunters exploited an Oracle PeopleSoft zero-day as a primary initial access vector across multiple 2026 breaches. Any organisation running Oracle PeopleSoft (common in universities, healthcare, government) should treat this as a critical vulnerability requiring immediate patching and audit.

Operating Model: Durable Cybercrime Franchise

Unlike traditional ransomware groups with a single leadership structure, ShinyHunters operates as a franchise: the brand, zero-day exploit infrastructure, and negotiation platform are centralised, while individual affiliate teams conduct breach operations independently. This makes law enforcement takedowns significantly harder — removing one team doesn't disrupt the franchise.

India Exposure

Canvas LMS breach affects Indian universities using Canvas. Match Group has Indian Tinder users. Abbott India is a listed subsidiary. Fluke Corporation supplies testing equipment to Indian industry. The Oracle PeopleSoft zero-day may affect Indian institutions (IITs, central universities, government departments) running PeopleSoft HCM/ERP.

Immediately check: Does your organisation use Oracle PeopleSoft? If yes — audit patch status, review recent unusual access events, check for new admin accounts, and engage an IR team. CERT-In has published Oracle PeopleSoft security advisories.
