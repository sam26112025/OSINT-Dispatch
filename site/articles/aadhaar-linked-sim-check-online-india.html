Aadhaar Linked SIM Check Online India — How to Verify and Report

The Hidden Danger of Unauthorised SIMs on Your Aadhaar

One of the most overlooked cyber threats facing Indians is the possibility of SIM cards registered against your Aadhaar without your knowledge. These unauthorised connections are weaponised by criminals for OTP fraud, anonymous WhatsApp scam accounts, UPI mule operations, and digital arrest extortion — all of which can be traced back to your identity, potentially implicating you in crimes you never committed. The government's Sanchar Saathi and TAFCOP portals give every citizen the power to check and shut down these connections. This guide explains exactly how.

Why Criminals Register SIMs on Your Aadhaar

Criminal UseHow Your Aadhaar SIM Enables It
OTP interception fraudReceive banking OTPs tied to your identity

How to Check SIMs Linked to Your Aadhaar — Step by Step

The primary method uses the TAFCOP portal on Sanchar Saathi:

  • Visit sancharsaathi.gov.in
  • Select "Know Your Mobile Connections" (TAFCOP)
  • Enter your active mobile number
  • Verify with the OTP sent to that number
  • View the complete list of connections registered to your Aadhaar
  • For any number you do not recognise, select "This is not my number" and submit a report

You can also call the DoT helpline 14422 for assistance with your registered connections.

Maximum SIM Limit Per Aadhaar in India

RegionMaximum SIMs Allowed
Most of India9 SIMs
Jammu & Kashmir6 SIMs
Northeast states & Assam6 SIMs

If your check reveals more connections than you actually hold, or numbers you never registered, those are unauthorised and must be reported immediately. Exceeding the legitimate limit is itself a fraud indicator.

How to Block Unauthorised SIMs

The reporting and blocking process works as follows:

  • Report each unauthorised SIM on TAFCOP with "This is not my number"
  • The DoT issues a notice to the relevant telecom operator
  • The operator must verify the connection or deactivate it within the prescribed timeline
  • For urgent cases, visit the telecom operator's store with your Aadhaar
  • If the SIM was used for fraud, file a complaint at cybercrime.gov.in

If Your Aadhaar Was Misused for Fraud

Where an unauthorised SIM has already been used in a crime, take comprehensive action:

  • File a complaint at cybercrime.gov.in and call 1930
  • Report to UIDAI at uidai.gov.in regarding the Aadhaar misuse
  • Lock your Aadhaar biometrics at myAadhaar.uidai.gov.in to prevent further authentication
  • Cite Section 66C IT Act (identity theft) and Section 318 BNS in your complaint
  • Preserve all evidence of the misuse for investigation

The CEIR Portal: Device-Level Protection

While TAFCOP addresses SIM fraud, the Central Equipment Identity Register (CEIR) at ceir.gov.in handles device fraud. If your phone is lost or stolen, CEIR lets you block the device by IMEI across all networks, preventing its misuse. Together, TAFCOP and CEIR provide complete coverage — TAFCOP for the SIM/connection layer and CEIR for the device layer.

Prevention: Protecting Your Aadhaar from SIM Fraud

Proactive habits dramatically reduce your risk:

  • Check TAFCOP every six months as routine digital hygiene
  • Lock your Aadhaar biometrics when not actively needed for KYC
  • Never share Aadhaar OTPs — UIDAI and telecom companies never ask for them by phone
  • Use a Virtual ID (VID) instead of your Aadhaar number where possible
  • Be cautious about where you submit Aadhaar copies, and mask them where permitted

Checking the SIMs linked to your Aadhaar takes only five minutes but provides powerful protection against having your identity weaponised in cybercrime. In an era where your Aadhaar is the master key to your digital and financial life, this simple check is one of the most important security habits every Indian can adopt.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I check how many SIM cards are registered on my Aadhaar?
Visit sancharsaathi.gov.in and use the 'Know Your Mobile Connections' feature. Enter your active mobile number, verify with OTP, and you will see all SIM cards registered to your Aadhaar across all operators.
What is the maximum number of SIM cards allowed per Aadhaar?
Maximum 9 SIM cards can be registered per Aadhaar across all operators in India. In Jammu & Kashmir and Northeast states, the limit is 6 SIMs. More than the limit indicates fraud.
What to do if someone has registered a SIM on my Aadhaar without my knowledge?
Report each unauthorized SIM on TAFCOP (sancharsaathi.gov.in), call DoT helpline 14422, file complaint on cybercrime.gov.in, and lock your Aadhaar biometrics at myAadhaar.uidai.gov.in to prevent further misuse.
