Business Email Compromise (BEC) Investigation India — Complete Guide

Business Email Compromise (BEC) is the costliest cybercrime globally, and India's corporate sector is increasingly targeted. Indian companies have lost crores to fake invoice fraud, CEO fraud, and vendor impersonation. This guide covers complete BEC investigation methodology.

BEC Attack Types

  • CEO Fraud: Fraudster impersonates CEO via spoofed email, instructs finance to wire transfer urgently
  • Invoice Fraud: Spoofed vendor email changes bank account details on invoice
  • Account Compromise: Real employee email account hacked, fraudulent payment instructions sent
  • Attorney Impersonation: Fake lawyer email instructs payment for legal settlement

Email Header Investigation

  • Check SPF, DKIM, DMARC — all should pass for legitimate emails
  • Reply-To address — often different from From address in BEC
  • X-Originating-IP — traces to attacker's mail server
  • Message-ID — unique identifier for tracking across mail servers

Domain Spoofing Analysis

  • Check exact domain — company.com vs c0mpany.com (zero instead of o)
  • Unicode homoglyph attacks — Cyrillic characters look identical to Latin
  • Subdomain spoofing — billing@payments.company.com (not official)
  • Check WHOIS for lookalike domain registration date

Recovery Steps

  1. Contact sending bank immediately — request SWIFT recall
  2. Contact receiving bank through SWIFT RCM (Recall/Return/Claim)
  3. File complaint at cybercrime.gov.in with transaction reference
  4. Engage law enforcement for international wire — ED under FEMA if foreign transfer

Legal Sections

S.66 IT Act (computer fraud), S.66C IT Act (identity theft), S.66D IT Act (personation using computer), S.318 BNS (cheating), S.420A Finance Act, FEMA violations for international transfers.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Business Email Compromise (BEC) fraud?
BEC fraud involves criminals impersonating executives or vendors via spoofed or hacked email accounts to trick employees into transferring funds or revealing credentials. India's corporate sector loses crores annually to CEO fraud and invoice fraud.
How to investigate a spoofed email in India?
Analyze email headers for SPF/DKIM/DMARC failures, check the Reply-To address, examine the exact sending domain for lookalike characters, and trace the originating IP. Report to CERT-In and file FIR under S.66D IT Act.
Can BEC fraud money be recovered in India?
Recovery is possible through immediate SWIFT recall if reported within hours. Contact your bank for SWIFT RCM (Recall Message) and the receiving bank's correspondent. International cases require MEA/ED intervention under FEMA.
