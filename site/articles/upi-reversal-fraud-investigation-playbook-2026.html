UPI Reversal Fraud — Investigation Playbook 2026

UPI payment reversal fraud is one of the fastest-growing cybercrime categories in India. The National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI) processes over 14 billion UPI transactions monthly — and a growing fraction involves fraudulent reversal requests, social engineering of OTPs, and mule account exploitation. This playbook provides investigators with a structured approach from complaint receipt to charge sheet.

Common UPI Fraud Variants

1. Fake Reversal Request

Fraudster sends money to victim claiming it was sent "by mistake." Victim is pressured to send it back via a collect/request that instead deducts from the victim's account.

2. QR Code Scam

Victim is told to "scan QR code to receive money." Scanning initiates a payment from victim's account instead.

3. OTP Phishing

Fraudster impersonates bank or NPCI, extracts OTP under the pretext of "account verification" or "UPI upgrade."

4. Fake Customer Care

Victim searches for bank/UPI customer care on Google. Fraudster's number appears in paid ads. Victim calls, shares credentials.

5. Merchant Fraud

Fraudster poses as buyer, generates fake payment screenshot, collects goods without actual payment.

Investigation Playbook

Step 1: Immediate Freezing (0-2 hours)

  • Victim calls 1930 — National Cyber Crime Helpline
  • File complaint on cybercrime.gov.in — obtain complaint number
  • Use complaint number to contact destination bank's nodal officer for immediate freeze
  • NPCI has a 24-hour helpline for LEA: contact through state cyber cell
  • Freeze window: RBI mandates banks to freeze within 4 hours of LEA request

Step 2: Transaction Trail

  • Obtain victim's bank statement — UPI transaction shows UTR (Unique Transaction Reference)
  • UTR number identifies originating and destination bank
  • Send Section 91 BNSS notice to both banks for complete transaction details
  • Request UPI app (PhonePe/GPay/Paytm) transaction logs via LEA portal

Step 3: OSINT on Suspect

  • UPI ID (VPA) reverse lookup — check linked mobile number via NPCI
  • Mobile number OSINT — TAFCOP, Truecaller, CDR request
  • Bank account OSINT — account opening KYC documents from bank
  • IP address from UPI app logs — geolocate and cross-reference with cell tower data

Step 4: Mule Account Network

UPI fraudsters almost always use mule accounts — bank accounts opened on others' KYC documents. Investigation must trace the full chain:

  • Layer 1: Victim account → First mule account
  • Layer 2: First mule → Multiple secondary mules
  • Layer 3: Cash withdrawal or crypto conversion

Request account opening documents from each mule bank. Most mules are recruited through job fraud — documenting the recruitment chain leads to the fraud ring organiser.

NPCI Dispute Resolution

For amounts under ₹10,000, direct NPCI dispute resolution through the UPI app may be faster than criminal proceedings. For larger amounts, LEA intervention is essential. The RBI Integrated Ombudsman Scheme covers UPI disputes not resolved by the payment service provider within 30 days.

Evidence for Court

  • Bank statement with UTR — certified copy with Section 63 BSA certificate
  • UPI app screenshot — hash the screenshot (SHA-256) at time of preservation
  • WhatsApp/SMS communications — export and hash
  • CCTV from ATM if cash withdrawal was made (subpoena from bank)
