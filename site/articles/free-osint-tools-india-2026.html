Frequently Asked Questions

What are the best free OSINT tools for India?
Best free OSINT tools for India include TAFCOP (SIM verification), MCA21 (company data), GST Search (business verification), eCourts (court records), Truecaller (phone lookup), VirusTotal (URL/IP checking), and the IGRS Intelligence Dashboard at igrs.xyz/dashboard.html.
How can I verify a business in India for free?
Visit MCA21 (mca.gov.in) for company registration and director details, GST Search (gst.gov.in) for GSTIN verification, and Zauba (zauba.com) for import/export data. All are free and require no registration.
Are there free tools to check if a website is fake?
Yes. Use VirusTotal (virustotal.com), URLScan (urlscan.io), Web-Check (check.igrs.xyz), and PhishTank (phishtank.org) to check websites for free. All provide threat intelligence without requiring an account.
