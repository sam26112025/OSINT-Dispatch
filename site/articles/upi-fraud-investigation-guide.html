Investigating UPI Fraud: Tracing Transactions, Mule Accounts and Recovery

File No.IGRS-018/2026
SubjectInvestigation methodology for UPI payment fraud cases in India
CategoryCyber Fraud
AuthorManuj Kumar Pandey
Published2026-04-14
Reading time7 minutes

UPI fraud is India's highest-volume cybercrime category. How transactions are traced, mule account networks mapped, and funds frozen — from the investigator's perspective.

The scale of UPI fraud

India's UPI processes over 13 billion transactions per month as of 2026. I4C data shows UPI-related fraud accounting for the largest share of cybercrime financial losses in the country. Fraud typologies range from simple QR code scams and fake payment confirmations to sophisticated vishing operations that manipulate victims into initiating transfers themselves.

The transaction trail

Every UPI transaction generates a Unique Transaction Reference (UTR) number — the primary forensic handle for any UPI fraud investigation. The UTR ties together the sender's VPA, recipient's VPA, amount, timestamp, and PSP bank on both ends. This trail is retained by NPCI and participating banks as a regulatory requirement.

The first investigative step: obtain the UTR from the victim and serve a legal notice on the recipient bank, requesting a hold on the beneficiary account and KYC disclosure. Speed is critical — funds in mule accounts cycle rapidly.

Mule account networks

Sophisticated UPI fraud moves through layers of mule accounts — temporarily holding points that cycle funds before reaching the fraudster via cash withdrawal, cryptocurrency exchange, or hawala. Mapping this network requires transaction data from multiple banks, which requires multiple legal notices or a court order compelling NPCI to provide the full transaction graph for the relevant UTRs.

Recovery rate: I4C reports fraud reported within one hour has significantly higher fund recovery rates than fraud reported after 24 hours. Most mule accounts clear within six to twelve hours.

The 1930 freeze mechanism

The 1930 helpline connects to CFCFRMS (Citizen Financial Cyber Fraud Reporting and Management System), which can flag accounts across all major banks simultaneously. The system sends alerts to recipient bank nodal officers, who can place a temporary administrative hold without a court order — executable within minutes of the report.

Building the case

UPI fraud prosecution requires demonstrating the transfer was unauthorised or induced by deception, identifying the ultimate beneficiary, and linking digital evidence to a physical person. The evidence chain — UTR records, KYC from banks, device registration data from PSPs, IMEI and IP logs — needs proper Section 63 certificates. Social engineering frauds additionally require evidence of the deceptive communication.
