I4C + Microsoft Block 1,000+ Skype IDs Used in Deepfake Digital Arrest Fraud Networks

By Manuj Kumar Pandey27 July 2026Cyber Fraud OperationsI4C Operation1000+ BlockedDeepfake Digital Arrest

India's I4C (Indian Cyber Crime Coordination Centre) has partnered with Microsoft to block over 1,000 Skype IDs used in deepfake digital arrest fraud operations — a major escalation in India's technical counter-measures against a fraud category that has caused ₹3,000 crore in losses and 92,000+ cases.

How the I4C-Microsoft Partnership Works

StepDetail
IdentificationSamanvaya platform aggregates victim complaints citing Skype IDs
Intelligence shareI4C flags accounts to Microsoft's Digital Crimes Unit
TakedownMicrosoft suspends account, preserves forensic data
EvidenceData preserved for MLAT requests — supports criminal prosecution

Why Scammers Chose Skype

Skype allows account creation with just an email — no mobile number, unlike WhatsApp. This makes it harder to trace via TRAI/telecom records. Skype's professional interface also added credibility to fake CBI/ED police station setups visible on victim video calls.

Scale: 92,000+ deepfake digital arrest cases in India (2025). ₹3,000 crore losses estimated by Supreme Court. A 60-second deepfake of a CBI officer now costs zero and takes under 25 minutes.

Frequently Asked Questions

How are Skype IDs traced and blocked in digital arrest cases?
I4C's Samanvaya platform aggregates Skype IDs from victim complaints, shares with Microsoft's Digital Crimes Unit, which suspends accounts and preserves forensic data for MLAT requests.
Why did fraudsters use Skype?
Skype registration requires only an email (not a mobile number), making it harder to trace via TRAI/telecom records. The professional interface added credibility to fake police station setups.
What is MLAT and how does it apply?
MLAT (Mutual Legal Assistance Treaty) allows India's CBI to formally request account data from foreign companies like Microsoft. Microsoft preserves login IPs, account creation details, and usage logs when served with a preservation request.
What should victims do?
Report immediately on 1930/cybercrime.gov.in with the Skype ID noted. This is now directly actionable via the I4C-Microsoft partnership. The 24-hour I4C fund freeze window is critical.
