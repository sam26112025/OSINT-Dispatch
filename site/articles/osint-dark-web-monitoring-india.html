Dark Web Monitoring for Indian Organizations: What to Watch and How

File No.IGRS-015/2026
SubjectOperational guide to dark web intelligence collection for Indian enterprise and law enforcement
CategoryThreat Intelligence
AuthorManuj Kumar Pandey
Published2026-04-05
Reading time6 minutes

Indian enterprises and government agencies are regularly featured in dark web data markets. A structured approach to monitoring what is being traded about your organization.

Why Indian organizations are high-value targets

India's digital expansion — UPI adoption, Aadhaar integration, and a booming startup ecosystem — has created data reservoirs enormously attractive to criminal markets. Breached databases containing Indian PAN numbers, Aadhaar-linked mobile numbers, bank account details and corporate credentials command premium prices on dark web forums.

What gets traded

The most common categories involving Indian organizations: employee credential dumps (email and password pairs from corporate SSO), customer PII databases (name, mobile, email, sometimes Aadhaar and PAN), payment card data from e-commerce breaches, network access listings (RDP credentials, VPN access), and "fullz" — complete identity packages combining PAN, Aadhaar, bank account, and mobile, used for financial fraud.

Monitoring architecture

  • Breach databases: Have I Been Pwned API for domain monitoring — any corporate email in a new breach triggers an alert
  • Paste monitoring: Automated keyword monitoring for your domain, executive names, and data patterns on Pastebin and equivalents
  • Threat intelligence feeds: Platforms like Recorded Future, Flashpoint, DarkOwl index dark web forum content; CERT-In's portal for budget-constrained teams
  • Forum monitoring: Requires proper OPSEC — dedicated hardware, Tor plus no-log VPN, sterile personas
Passive monitoring is legally straightforward. Active infiltration of criminal markets — impersonating buyers, making purchases — requires law enforcement authorization.

Response when you find your data

Verify the data is genuine and assess recency. Notify affected individuals per DPDP Act 2023. Rotate compromised credentials organization-wide. Engage CERT-In if volume or sensitivity warrants. Preserve evidence forensically with timestamps and seller identifiers for law enforcement referral.
