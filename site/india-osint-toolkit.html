> INDIA OSINT IDENTIFIER TOOLKIT_

Five India-specific identifier validators built for cyber cells, law-enforcement units, fraud teams and corporate security. Validate the format and checksum of a PAN, GSTIN, IFSC, UPI handle or Aadhaar number during triage — entirely inside your browser. Nothing you type is uploaded, logged or looked up.

How to read these tools: they confirm whether an identifier is well-formed and (where applicable) passes its public checksum. A "valid" result does not confirm a real person, a live registration, or ownership. For authoritative verification always use the official portal, and handle personal data lawfully under the DPDP Act and due legal process.
PAN Validator & Decoder

A Permanent Account Number is ten characters: five letters, four digits, one letter. The 4th letter encodes the holder type and the 5th encodes the first letter of the holder's surname or entity name. This tool checks the structure and decodes those two characters.

GSTIN Validator & Decoder

A GST Identification Number is 15 characters: a 2-digit state code, the holder's 10-character PAN, a 1-character entity number, a default "Z", and a final checksum digit. This tool verifies the structure, decodes the state and embedded PAN, and recomputes the official mod-36 checksum.

IFSC Parser

An Indian Financial System Code is 11 characters: the first four identify the bank, the fifth is always "0" (reserved), and the last six identify the branch. This tool validates the structure and links out to authoritative branch lookups. The Reserve Bank of India is the issuing authority.

UPI Handle Decoder

A UPI Virtual Payment Address looks like name@bank. The part after the "@" is the PSP/bank handle and often reveals which payment app or bank issued it. This tool validates the format and maps the handle to its provider where known — useful when triaging UPI-fraud complaints.

Aadhaar Format Checker (Verhoeff)

A genuine Aadhaar number is 12 digits and its last digit is a Verhoeff check digit over the first eleven. This tool confirms the length, that it does not start with 0 or 1, and that the Verhoeff checksum is consistent — catching typos and obviously fabricated numbers during document triage.

Privacy: this check is fully local and offline. The number you type never leaves your browser, is never stored, and is never sent to UIDAI or any service. This is a format check only — it does not confirm a real holder. Handle Aadhaar data strictly under the DPDP Act and lawful process.

Why identifier validation matters in Indian investigations

In Indian cyber-fraud and financial investigations, analysts are handed dozens of identifiers from FIRs, complaint portals, screenshots and seized devices — PANs, GST numbers, bank codes, UPI handles and Aadhaar references. Many are mistyped, partially redacted, or deliberately fabricated. A fast, offline format-and-checksum check lets an investigator separate plausible identifiers from obvious noise before spending time on authoritative verification or raising formal requests.

Validation is a triage step, not proof. A correctly structured GSTIN or a checksum-valid Aadhaar simply means the number could be real; it still has to be confirmed through the official channel and, where personal data is involved, through lawful process. Used this way, these tools speed up the early "is this even worth chasing" decision while keeping the evidentiary standard intact.

Frequently asked questions

Does this tool look up or store anything I enter?

No. Every check runs inside your browser using format rules and public checksum algorithms. Nothing is transmitted, logged or stored — you can confirm this by using the tools offline.

Does a "valid" result confirm a real person or registration?

No. It only confirms the number is well-formed and passes its checksum. Confirmation of a real holder or live registration requires the official government portal.

Where do I verify these officially?

PAN — the Income Tax e-filing portal; GSTIN — the GST portal's "Search Taxpayer"; IFSC — the issuing bank or RBI; Aadhaar — UIDAI, strictly under lawful authority. This toolkit never substitutes for those sources.

Is the Aadhaar checker safe and legal to use?

It performs a local, offline Verhoeff format check only and never contacts UIDAI. Investigators must still handle Aadhaar data lawfully under the DPDP Act and obtain data through proper process.
