What IGRS Is

IGRS — the Intel Grid Research Station — is a free, no-login intelligence platform built for India’s frontline defenders: investigators, law-enforcement cyber cells, intelligence units, corporate security teams, and the students who will join them. It brings open-source intelligence, threat intelligence, and digital-forensics resources together in one place, organised around how real investigations are conducted rather than around vendor categories.

What you will find here

The platform combines a curated directory of several hundred OSINT tools, a categorised library of search operators for authorised reconnaissance, investigation playbooks, a quick-reference to India’s cyber laws, and a working analyst dashboard — alongside a growing body of original articles on fraud investigation, threat intelligence, and digital forensics. Everything is designed to be useful in the field and accessible without an account or paywall.

Built for the Indian context

What sets the material apart is its grounding in Indian law and practice: the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, the Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, and the Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam of 2023, the Information Technology Act, 2000, and the official portals — the 1930 helpline, the National Cybercrime Reporting Portal, CERT-In, and others — that an Indian investigator actually relies on. The aim is to democratise capability that has too often been locked behind cost and complexity.
