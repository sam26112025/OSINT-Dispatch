Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how IGRS ("we", "us", "this website"), accessible at https://igrs.xyz, collects, uses and protects information when you visit this website. By using this website, you agree to the practices described here.

Information we collect

We do not require registration and do not knowingly collect personal information except in the following cases:

  • Contact form: if you contact us, we receive the name, email address and message you voluntarily provide. This information is used only to respond to your enquiry and is not sold or shared for marketing.

Cookies

Cookies are small files stored on your device by your browser. This website and its third-party partners may use cookies to understand site usage and, where advertising is enabled, to serve relevant advertisements. You can disable cookies through your browser settings at any time; the website will remain functional.

  • Users may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or opt out of third-party vendor cookies at aboutads.info.

Analytics

We may use privacy-respecting analytics or Google Analytics to understand aggregate traffic patterns (pages visited, approximate region, device type). This data is aggregated and is not used to identify individual visitors.

Third-party links

Articles may link to external websites. We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of third-party sites and encourage you to review their policies.

Children's privacy

This website is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and it will be deleted.

Data retention and your rights

Contact-form correspondence is retained only as long as needed to handle the enquiry. Depending on your jurisdiction (including under the GDPR and India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023), you may have rights to access, correct or request deletion of personal data you have provided. To exercise these rights, contact us at contact@igrs.xyz.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
