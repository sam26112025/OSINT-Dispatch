> OSINT INVESTIGATION PLAYBOOKS_

Step-by-step investigation SOPs for cybercrime investigators, corporate security teams, and defence analysts. Each playbook covers tools, methodology, evidence collection, and legal compliance specific to India.

Why Investigation Playbooks Matter

A playbook is a standard operating procedure for a specific type of investigation — a documented, repeatable sequence of steps from first report to final reporting. Under the pressure of a live case, structured procedure is what prevents missed evidence, broken chains of custody, and inconsistent outcomes between analysts. The discipline a playbook imposes is also what makes a conclusion defensible when it is later examined by a senior officer, a court, or opposing counsel.

How to use the playbooks on this page

Follow the sequence rather than improvising, and document each step as you complete it — what you did, when, and what you found. Adapt the procedure to the facts of your case, but record any deviation and the reason for it. The goal is an investigation that another competent analyst could reproduce from your notes and arrive at the same result.

Anchored in Indian law and procedure

Each playbook is written for the Indian legal environment. Where evidence must be obtained from an intermediary or service provider, production is sought under Section 94 of the Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023. Electronic evidence is accompanied by a certificate under Section 63 of the Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023. Substantive offences are framed under the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023 and the Information Technology Act, 2000. Building these references into the procedure itself keeps the investigation aligned with what a prosecution will ultimately require.

Why Standardised Investigation Playbooks Matter

The difference between an investigation that holds up and one that falls apart is rarely a single brilliant insight — it is method. A documented playbook turns an individual's experience into a repeatable process, so that two analysts working the same lead arrive at comparable, defensible results. That consistency is what lets a team scale, onboard new investigators quickly, and avoid the silent errors that creep in when every case is improvised from scratch.

Defensibility and the record

Standardised procedures also protect the evidentiary value of the work. When each step — collection, preservation, hashing, analysis, reporting — follows a known sequence, the chain of custody is intelligible to a court, and findings can be paired with a certificate under Section 63 of the Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023 that governs electronic evidence in India. A playbook makes the methodology auditable: a reviewer can see not just what was concluded, but how, and can reproduce it. These SOPs are written for that purpose — practical, field-tested sequences that keep an investigation both efficient and admissible, rather than fast but fragile.
